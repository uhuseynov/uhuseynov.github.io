Hi, I am a **Computer Science** student with a strong interest in **Cybersecurity and Artificial Intelligence**, particularly in how intelligent systems can support security monitoring and detection.

I am currently focused on building a solid foundation in core cybersecurity concepts, including system security, network fundamentals, threat analysis, and defensive security practices. Alongside this, I am interested in how AI and automation can be applied to areas such as log analysis, anomaly detection, and security operations.

This blog serves as my personal learning journal, where I document my progress, share technical insights, and explore real-world cybersecurity scenarios. I use it as a way to solidify what I learn and track my growth over time.

My interest in cybersecurity comes from understanding how attacks work and how defenders can detect and respond to them effectively. I especially enjoy analyzing real-world attack scenarios related to user account abuse, brute-force attacks, credential misuse, and suspicious authentication behavior.

I actively practice my skills using platforms such as **TryHackMe, HackTheBox, and PortSwigger**, where I work on hands-on labs and beginner-to-intermediate security challenges. I am also gradually exploring how *AI-driven* approaches can enhance detection and analysis in these scenarios.

This blog is my personal learning space where I share:

- SOC-related notes

- Write-ups from labs

- Beginner-friendly cybersecurity concepts

- Real-world attack detection and analysis

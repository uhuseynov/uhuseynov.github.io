About Me#

Hi, I am a Computer Science student with a strong interest in Cybersecurity.
I am currently focused on developing my skills as a Junior SOC Analyst, with particular attention to Windows Security Logs, log analysis, and basic incident detection techniques.

My interest in cybersecurity comes from understanding how attacks work and how defenders can detect and respond to them effectively. I enjoy analyzing real-world attack scenarios, especially those related to user account abuse, brute-force attacks, and credential misuse.

I actively practice my skills using platforms such as TryHackMe, where I work on hands-on labs and beginner-to-intermediate security challenges.

This blog is my personal learning journal where I share:

  • SOC-related notes
  • Windows Event Log analysis
  • TryHackMe write-ups
  • Beginner-friendly cybersecurity concepts
